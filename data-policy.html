Data Policy

Last Updated: January 2026

1. Information Collection

We collect information that you provide directly to us, including contact details, company information, and service preferences. We also collect information about leads and prospects as part of our service delivery.

2. Information Usage

Information is used to provide the services requested by our clients, communicate about service matters, and improve our services. We do not use information for unrelated purposes without consent.

3. Lead and Prospect Data

Lead and prospect information is collected and used solely for the purpose of providing agreed-upon services to clients. Data handling practices are established in accordance with client agreements and applicable requirements.

4. Data Security Measures

We implement reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to protect information. These include access controls, encryption where appropriate, and regular security assessments.

5. Data Access

Access to personal and lead information is limited to authorized personnel who require access to perform their job functions. All personnel are bound by confidentiality obligations.

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell personal information or lead data for unrelated purposes. We may share information with service providers who assist us in operating our business, provided they are bound by confidentiality and data protection obligations.

7. Data Retention

We retain information only as long as necessary for legitimate business purposes, legal requirements, or as agreed with clients. When information is no longer needed, we take reasonable steps to delete or anonymize it.

8. Client Data Responsibilities

Clients are responsible for ensuring that any information they provide to us is accurate and that they have the necessary rights to share such information. Clients are also responsible for the secure handling of leads delivered to them.

9. Data Subject Rights

Individuals may request access to, correction of, or deletion of their personal information by contacting us. We will respond to such requests in accordance with applicable laws and our contractual obligations.

10. International Data Transfers

If we transfer information outside of the country where it was collected, we ensure appropriate safeguards are in place to protect the information in accordance with applicable data protection laws.

11. Policy Updates

We may update this Data Policy from time to time. We will notify clients of material changes and post the updated policy on our website with an updated effective date.

12. Contact Information

For questions about our Data Policy or our data practices, please contact us through the contact form on our website.
